# MermaidDiagrams

Reads [Mermaid](https://mermaid.js.org) diagram text, lays the diagram out and draws it with Core Graphics and Core Text, in the visual style of [Pele](https://github.com/obsidianmd/pele), Obsidian's Mermaid renderer. It knows no app: fonts, colors and limits are parameters, and it reads and writes no file.

Graphite's plan for it is `Docs/Native-diagrams-todo.md`; the decisions that shaped it are recorded there.

## Diagram types

| Type | State |
| --- | --- |
| Flowchart (`flowchart`, `graph`) | Drawn. Laid out in ranks, subgraphs as nested boxes in their own directions. Read: the direction, nodes in the classic shapes (by bracket or `@{ shape: … }`), quoted and Markdown text, entity codes, edges with their strokes, marks, lengths and labels, chains and `&`, groups nested as Mermaid nests them, `accTitle` and `accDescr`. Styles and `click` are counted and not applied; a collapsed subgraph is refused. |
| State diagram (`stateDiagram`, `stateDiagram-v2`) | Drawn, without composite states yet. Read: states with their texts (`Id : text`, `state "text" as Id`), transitions with their text, `[*]` starts and ends, `<<choice>>`, `<<fork>>` and `<<join>>`, notes (`note left of`, `note right of`, on one line or up to `end note`), composite bodies with their regions (`--`), `direction`, `accTitle` and `accDescr`. Styles (`classDef`, `class`, `:::`, `style`) and `click` are counted and not applied; floating notes are read and not drawn, as in Mermaid. |
| Class diagram (`classDiagram`, `classDiagram-v2`) | Drawn. Laid out in ranks, without namespaces yet. Read: classes with labels, generic types, annotations and members (visibility, static, abstract, return types), every relation mark on either side with cardinalities and text, lollipop interfaces, notes, the direction, `accTitle` and `accDescr`. Styles, `click`, `link` and `callback` are counted and not applied. |
| Sequence diagram (`sequenceDiagram`) | Drawn, in Pele's measurements. Read: participants and actors with aliases, every arrow (`->`, `-->`, `->>`, `-->>`, `-x`, `--x`, `-)`, `--)`, `<<->>`, `<<-->>`), activations, notes, `loop`, `alt`, `opt`, `par`, `critical`, `break` and `rect` blocks, `autonumber`, `title`, `accTitle` and `accDescr`. `box`, `create`, `destroy`, participant menus and attributes, half arrows and central connections are read and not laid out. |
| Entity relationship (`erDiagram`) | Drawn. Laid out in ranks, without subgraphs yet. Read: entities by bare, quoted or numeric name, aliases, attribute blocks (type with `?`, name, `PK`, `FK` and `UK` keys, comment; backticks and `~` generics), relationships with every cardinality in symbols or words, identifying and non-identifying lines and labels, `direction`, `accTitle` and `accDescr`. Styles and classes are counted and not applied. |
| Mindmap (`mindmap`) | Drawn, Pele's way: the root in the middle, branches to its right and left in the series colors. Read: the tree from indentation, every shape (`[ ]`, `( )`, `(( ))`, `) (`, `)) ((`, `{{ }}`, none), quoted and Markdown text, `%%` comments, `accTitle` and `accDescr` (not in Mermaid's mindmap grammar). Icons and classes are counted and not drawn. |
| Timeline (`timeline`) | Drawn, Pele's way: across, or down for `timeline TD`, periods and sections in the series colors. Read: `title`, `section`, periods with their events on their line and the lines after, `accTitle` and `accDescr`, `%%` and `#` comments; only `<br>` is markup in the text. Front matter configuration (`disableMulticolor`) is not read. |
| Gantt chart (`gantt`) | Drawn: a row per task (bars, milestones), section bands and names, excluded days, today's line, the axis' dates. Read and scheduled as Mermaid does: `dateFormat`, `axisFormat`, `tickInterval`, `weekday`, `inclusiveEndDates`, `topAxis`, `excludes`, `includes`, `weekend`, `todayMarker`, `title`, `accTitle`, `accDescr`, sections, tasks with `done`, `active`, `crit`, `milestone`, identifiers, `after`, `until`, dates and durations. `click` and `vert` are read and refused (`GanttLayoutError.statementNotDrawn`). |
| Pie chart (`pie`) | Drawn: slices clockwise from the top in the series colors, percentages inside or outside the rim, the legend on the right, the title above. Read: `showData`, `title`, `accTitle`, `accDescr`, slices (`"label" : value`). The front matter's `config` options are not applied. |
| Git graph (`gitGraph`) | Drawn, Pele's way: a lane per branch in the series colors, commits as dots, squares, crossed dots, rings and cherries, ids turned an eighth of a turn, tags, branch names, Pele's curves between lanes; across, or down (`TB:`) or up (`BT:`). Read and played as Mermaid's database plays them: `commit` with `id:`, `msg:`, `tag:` and `type:`, `branch` with `order:`, `checkout` and `switch`, `merge` with `id:`, `tag:` and `type:`, `cherry-pick` with `id:`, `tag:` and `parent:`, `title`, `accTitle`, `accDescr`; what Mermaid refuses is a typed `GitGraphSyntaxProblem`, a repeated commit id included. A graph whose front matter or directive sets `gitGraph` options is refused (`GitGraphLayoutError.optionsNotApplied`). |
| User journey (`journey`) | Drawn, Pele's way across the page: cards under their sections' bands, a scale from 5 to 1 with a happy, neutral or sad face per task at its score and a line from face to face, the actors' dots in the series colors and their legend. Read: `title`, `section`, tasks (`Task: score: actor, actor`, the score read as JavaScript's `Number` reads it), `accTitle`, `accDescr`, `%%` and `#` comments; only `<br>` is markup in the text. Pele's turn to a column when the page is narrow is not done. |
| Quadrant chart (`quadrantChart`) | Drawn, Pele's way on a page of 500 points: the four quadrants with their texts, the axes' texts (the y axis's turned), the points at their coordinates in their styles and their classes', each text at the first free place around its point, the title. Read: `title`, `x-axis` and `y-axis` with their low and high texts, `quadrant-1` to `quadrant-4`, points (`Name:::class: [x, y] radius: 10, color: #ff3300, stroke-color: #10f0f0, stroke-width: 5px`), `classDef`, `accTitle`, `accDescr`, `%%` comments. The front matter's `config` and theme variables are not applied. |
| XY chart (`xychart`, `xychart-beta`) | Drawn, Pele's way: bars and lines in the series colors over a grid, the axes with their marks and titles, the legend on the right; `horizontal` turns the axes. Read as Mermaid's grammar reads it: `vertical` or `horizontal`, `title`, `x-axis` (a title, then categories in brackets or a range `a --> b`), `y-axis` (a title, then a range), `bar` and `line` with their values and a line's point labels, `accTitle`, `accDescr`, `%%` comments. The front matter's `config` options (`showDataLabel`, `width`, `height`, `chartOrientation`) and theme colors are not applied. |
| Sankey diagram (`sankey`, `sankey-beta`) | Drawn, Pele's way: nodes as bars in columns in the series colors, bands between them in a gradient from their source's color to their target's, each node's name and value beside its bar. Read as Mermaid's grammar reads it: rows of `source,target,value`, plain or quoted (commas, line breaks and doubled quotation marks inside quotation marks), `%%` comment lines, values as `parseFloat` reads them. The front matter's `config` options `width`, `height`, `nodeWidth`, `nodePadding`, `showValues`, `prefix`, `suffix` and `nodeAlignment` are applied; `labelStyle: outlined`, `linkColor` and `nodeColors` are not drawn yet, and a diagram that sets them, or whose links run in a circle, is not laid out. |

## Public API

- `MermaidDiagramReader.read(_:limits:)` reads a diagram's text and returns a `MermaidDiagram` (one case per type read), or throws a `MermaidDiagramReadError`: no diagram, a type not read yet (by its keyword), a syntax problem with its line (`MermaidSyntaxProblem`), or a limit passed.
- `MermaidDiagramLimits`: the bounds a diagram is read and laid out within.
- `FlowchartLayoutEngine.layout(_:typography:limits:)` lays a flowchart out and returns a `FlowchartLayout`: its size, and each node's shape, frame and text, each edge's path, marks and text, and the title and the groups' boxes (`FlowchartLayoutCluster`), on a page whose y runs down. It throws `FlowchartLayoutError` for subgraphs it does not lay out (a collapsed group, an edge between a group and what it holds, two exits across a box's flow from one rank) or past the layout limit.
- `DiagramTypography(font:)`: the measurers of a `DiagramFont` (the system font or one by PostScript name), made once and used for every diagram in that font. `CoreTextDiagramTextMeasurer` measures with Core Text; `DiagramTextMeasuring` is what the layout measures with.
- `DiagramTextBlock`, `DiagramPath` (with `DiagramPathSegment`) and `DiagramEdgeMark`: placed text, lines and edge marks.
- `FlowchartDrawingParts(layout:)` groups a layout's geometry by how it is painted: Core Graphics paths for the groups' boxes (`FlowchartClusterDrawingParts`: fills by depth, outlines, titles, and the patches of titles edges cross), the solid, thick and dotted edges, the filled and stroked marks, the patches behind edge text, the node fills and the node outlines, with the text blocks; a view paints them with its own shapes and colors.
- `FlowchartRenderer.draw(_:in:palette:)` paints those parts into a `CGContext` whose y runs down, in a `DiagramPalette` (Pele's color roles: background, foreground, line, surface, border, alternate surface for boxes, muted for their titles). It parses and measures nothing, so a change of colors only draws again.
- `MindmapLayoutEngine.layout(_:typography:)` lays a mindmap out (`MindmapLayout`: each node's outline, frame, text and branch, each link's path and branch, the title); `MindmapDrawingParts(layout:)` groups it by branch for painting, and `MindmapRenderer.draw(_:in:palette:)` paints it, each branch in `DiagramPalette.seriesColor(_:)`. `MindmapDiagram`, `MindmapNode` and `MindmapNodeShape` are a mindmap as its text describes it.
- `TimelineLayoutEngine.layout(_:typography:)` lays a timeline out (`TimelineLayout`: sections, periods with their dots, lines and events, the axis and its arrow, the title); `TimelineDrawingParts(layout:)` groups it by color, and `TimelineRenderer.draw(_:in:palette:)` paints it. `TimelineDiagram`, `TimelinePeriod` and `TimelineDirection` are a timeline as its text describes it.
- `FlowchartDiagram`, `FlowchartNode`, `FlowchartNodeShape`, `FlowchartEdge` (with `FlowchartEdgeStroke` and `FlowchartArrowhead`), `FlowchartSubgraph` (its nodes, nested groups, direction and the node standing for it), `FlowchartDirection`: a flowchart as its text describes it.
- `DiagramLabel`: the text of a node, an edge or a group as plain lines, drawn as the characters they hold.
- `StateDiagramLayoutEngine.layout(_:typography:limits:)` lays a state diagram out and returns a `StateDiagramLayout`: its size, each state's kind, frame, name and other descriptions, each transition's path, arrow and text, each note's frame, text and line to its state, and the title. It throws `StateDiagramLayoutError` for a diagram with composite states or one past the layout limit.
- `StateDiagramDrawingParts(layout:)` groups a state diagram's geometry by how it is painted, and `StateDiagramRenderer.draw(_:in:palette:)` paints it into a `CGContext`, as the flowchart's do; a note's text is `DiagramPalette.noteText`, between the background and the text.
- `StateDiagram`, `StateDiagramState` (with `StateDiagramStateKind`), `StateDiagramTransition`, `StateDiagramNote` (with `StateDiagramNoteSide`), `StateDiagramComposite`: a state diagram as its text describes it.
- `ClassDiagramLayoutEngine.layout(_:typography:limits:)` lays a class diagram out and returns a `ClassDiagramLayout`: each class's box with its compartment lines, annotations, name and members, the notes, the interfaces' names, each relation's path, marks, text and cardinalities, and the title. It throws `ClassDiagramLayoutError` for a diagram with namespaces or one past the layout limit. `ClassDiagramDrawingParts(layout:)` groups its geometry by how it is painted, and `ClassDiagramRenderer.draw(_:in:palette:)` paints it in a `ClassDiagramPalette` (a `DiagramPalette` and Pele's muted and note colors).
- `ClassDiagram`, `ClassDiagramClass`, `ClassDiagramMember` (with `ClassDiagramMemberClassifier`), `ClassDiagramRelation` (with `ClassDiagramRelationEndpoint`, `ClassDiagramRelationEnd` and `ClassDiagramLineStyle`), `ClassDiagramNote` and `ClassDiagramNamespace`: a class diagram as its text describes it.
- `SequenceLayoutEngine.layout(_:typography:)` lays a `SequenceDiagram` out and returns a `SequenceLayout` (participants above and below their lifelines, lifelines, highlights, activations, block frames, messages, notes and the title), or throws `SequenceLayoutError` for a diagram holding statements not laid out yet (`SequenceDiagram.statementsNotLaidOut`). `SequenceDrawingParts(layout:)` and `SequenceRenderer.draw(_:in:palette:)` paint it as the flowchart's parts and renderer do, with Pele's muted text and second surface mixed between the palette's background and foreground.
- `EntityRelationshipLayoutEngine.layout(_:typography:limits:)` lays an entity relationship diagram out and returns an `EntityRelationshipLayout` (each entity's box with its name and attribute rows, each relationship's path, cardinality marks and label, the title), or throws `EntityRelationshipLayoutError` for one with subgraphs or past the layout limit. `EntityRelationshipDrawingParts(layout:)` groups its geometry by how it is painted, and `EntityRelationshipRenderer.draw(_:in:palette:)` paints it, types, keys and comments in the text color at `EntityRelationshipDrawingParts.mutedTextOpacity`.
- `EntityRelationshipDiagram`, `EntityRelationshipEntity`, `EntityRelationshipAttribute` (with `EntityRelationshipAttributeKey`), `EntityRelationship` (with `EntityRelationshipCardinality` and `EntityRelationshipIdentification`): an entity relationship diagram as its text describes it.
- `GanttChart` (with `GanttTask`): a Gantt chart as its text describes it, dates as written. `GanttLayoutEngine.layout(_:typography:now:timeZone:limits:)` schedules it on the moment and in the time zone given and returns a `GanttLayout` (the title, and the plot: rows, bars and milestones with their paint, text, section bands, excluded days, grid, axis dates, today's line), or throws `GanttLayoutError`; `GanttDrawingParts(layout:)` and `GanttRenderer.draw(_:in:palette:)` paint it, in `DiagramPalette`'s accent, critical, muted and second surface colors.
- `GitGraphDiagram` (with `GitGraphCommit`, `GitGraphBranch`, `GitGraphCommitKind`, `GitGraphCommitType` and `GitGraphDirection`): a Git graph once its statements are played, commits in the order they were made with their branch and parents, branches in lane order. `GitGraphLayoutEngine.layout(_:typography:)` returns a `GitGraphLayout` (lanes with their lines and names, connectors with their style, commits with their symbol, turned id and tags, the title), or throws `GitGraphLayoutError` for a graph that sets options; `GitGraphDrawingParts(layout:)` groups it by series color, and `GitGraphRenderer.draw(_:in:palette:)` paints it.
- `UserJourneyDiagram` (with `UserJourneyTask` and `UserJourneyMood`): a user journey as its text describes it. `UserJourneyLayoutEngine.layout(_:typography:)` returns a `UserJourneyLayout` (the section bands, each task's card, text, face, stem and actors' dots, the scale's levels, the line from face to face, the legend, the title); `UserJourneyDrawingParts(layout:)` groups it by how it is painted, the dots by series color; `UserJourneyRenderer.draw(_:in:palette:)` paints it.
- `QuadrantChart` (with `QuadrantChartPoint`, `QuadrantChartPointStyle` and `QuadrantChartColor`): a quadrant chart as its text describes it. `QuadrantChartLayoutEngine.layout(_:typography:)` returns a `QuadrantChartLayout` (the quadrants' boxes and texts, the axes' texts, turned or not, the points with their paint, outline and placed text, the title); `QuadrantChartDrawingParts(layout:)` groups it by how it is painted, the points back to front by paint; `QuadrantChartRenderer.draw(_:in:palette:)` paints it, a point in `DiagramPalette.seriesColor(at: 0)` unless the chart writes its color.
- `PieChart` (with `PieChartSlice`): a pie chart as its text describes it. `PieChartLayoutEngine.layout(_:typography:)` returns a `PieChartLayout` (the pie's center and radius, each drawn slice's angles and percentage, the legend's swatches and labels, the title); `PieChartDrawingParts(layout:)` groups it by how it is painted, one fill per series color; `PieChartRenderer.draw(_:in:palette:)` paints it, the slices in `DiagramPalette.series`.
- `XYChart` (with `XYChartXAxis`, `XYChartXAxisScale`, `XYChartYAxis`, `XYChartValueRange`, `XYChartSeries` and `XYChartOrientation`): an XY chart as Mermaid's database holds it, each series' values placed along the x axis. `XYChartLayoutEngine.layout(_:typography:)` returns an `XYChartLayout` (the plot's frame, the grid, the axes and their labels and titles, the bars, the lines' runs and dots, the point labels, the legend, the title) or throws `XYChartLayoutError.noSeries`; `XYChartDrawingParts(layout:)` groups it by how it is painted, and `XYChartRenderer.draw(_:in:palette:)` paints it, the series in `DiagramPalette.series`.
- `SankeyDiagram` (with `SankeyNode`, `SankeyLink`, `SankeyOptions` and `SankeyNodeAlignment`): a Sankey diagram as its rows and its front matter's options describe it. `SankeyLayoutEngine.layout(_:typography:)` returns a `SankeyLayout` (each node's bar, each link's band, each node's label, the title) or throws `SankeyLayoutError` (circular links, or options not drawn yet); `SankeyDrawingParts(layout:)` groups it by how it is painted, and `SankeyRenderer.draw(_:in:palette:)` paints it, each band a gradient between two of `DiagramPalette.series`.

## Limits

`MermaidDiagramLimits.standard` bounds the text to 50,000 UTF-8 bytes (Mermaid's default), a diagram to 5,000 nodes (a state diagram's states and notes together) and 5,000 edges (Pele's edge limit; `A & B --> C & D` makes four edges, so a short text can ask for many; a pie chart's slices, a Gantt chart's tasks, a Git graph's commits, branches and tags, a user journey's sections and tasks and a quadrant chart's points and classes count as nodes, and so do an XY chart's categories and values together; the actors named on a user journey's tasks count as edges, and a Sankey diagram's nodes and links as nodes and edges), the layout to 50,000 points, and a Gantt chart's scheduling to 500,000 days stepped through. Each count is checked before anything of that count is made, and a diagram past a limit fails with a typed error.

## Layout

A layered layout (Sugiyama's method) on plain values: the edges that close cycles are turned around, nodes get ranks by their longest path (a node no edge enters moves down next to its successor), an edge spanning several ranks passes through a point in each rank between, an edge's text gets a rank of its own between the ranks of nodes, the order across each rank comes from sweeps that sort by the neighbours' mean position, keeping the order with the fewest crossings, and positions across come from a weighted isotonic regression per rank, solved exactly by pooling adjacent violators, then straightened along long edges. The measurements are Pele's: 40 points between nodes, 48 between ranks, 18 and 12 points of padding around a node's text, text wrapped at 200 points and an edge's text seven eighths of the nodes'. Edges meeting a side of a node spread along it, 20 points apart where the side has room, and meet the shape's outline.

A state diagram is laid out the same way, with the measurements of Pele's state renderer: 44 points between ranks, 16 and 10 points of padding around a state's name, starts first in the order, then the states in the order transitions first name them. Transitions to a state itself loop beside it, and notes sit beside their state across the flow, a rank gap away; a state's place in its rank takes both on each side, so that its transitions still meet it in the middle.

Subgraphs are laid out as Pele's compound layout does (`LayeredClusterLayout`): each box on its own, in ranks, in its own direction, from the deepest up, then placed in the box around it as one node of its size, with 20 points of padding and a band for its title. An edge crossing a border passes through a point of it on the side facing where it goes: a border point ranked before or after the box's content on the sides its flow starts from or ends at, or straight across the flow from a node pinned first or last in its rank; outside, the edge is held at that point. A subgraph keeps its own direction when it links outside, as in Pele.

## What the package does not do

It imports Foundation, CoreGraphics and CoreText only: no UIKit, AppKit or SwiftUI. It reads and writes no file.

## Tests and benchmark

`swift test --package-path Packages/MermaidDiagrams`. `FlowchartCorpusTests` reads and lays out Mermaid's 114 flowchart documentation examples and Pele's generated trees and meshes; `ClassDiagramCorpusTests` Mermaid's 38 class diagram documentation examples, Pele's four-class diagram and generated hierarchies; `SequenceCorpusTests` reads Mermaid's 38 sequence documentation examples and lays out the 24 without statements not laid out; `EntityRelationshipCorpusTests` Mermaid's 25 entity relationship examples and generated schemas; `MindmapCorpusTests` Mermaid's 13 mindmap examples and generated trees; `TimelineCorpusTests` Mermaid's 14 timeline examples and generated timelines; `GitGraphCorpusTests` Mermaid's 35 Git graph examples (the 26 without options drawn) and generated histories; `UserJourneyCorpusTests` Mermaid's user journey example and generated journeys; `QuadrantChartCorpusTests` Mermaid's 3 quadrant chart examples and generated charts; `XYChartCorpusTests` Mermaid's 8 XY chart examples and generated charts; `SankeyCorpusTests` Mermaid's 8 Sankey examples, the spec's energy flows and generated flows.

`swift run -c release --package-path Packages/MermaidDiagrams MermaidDiagramsBenchmark` times reading, layout, the drawing parts and painting, with the median and 99th percentile per diagram, and the heap a layout holds, on the inputs of Pele's benchmark (`FlowchartCorpus`; the documentation examples are its resources), then mindmaps (`MindmapBenchmark` on `MindmapCorpus`), timelines (`TimelineBenchmark` on `TimelineCorpus`), Git graphs (`GitGraphBenchmark` on `GitGraphCorpus`), user journeys (`UserJourneyBenchmark` on `UserJourneyCorpus`), quadrant charts (`QuadrantChartBenchmark` on `QuadrantChartCorpus`), XY charts (`XYChartBenchmark` on `XYChartCorpus`) and Sankey diagrams (`SankeyBenchmark` on `SankeyCorpus`), among the other types. Graphite records the results in its `Docs/Coverage.md`.
